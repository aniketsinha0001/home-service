How it works

Our process

Five stages, each with a clear output, so you always know what has been decided and what comes next.

  1. Consultation

    We visit the site, take measurements and learn your needs and budget.

  2. Concept and layout

    You review space plans and mood boards, and we refine them together.

  3. Design and materials

    3D views, material samples and a detailed quotation for approval.

  4. Execution

    Our team builds on site with regular updates and photos.

  5. Handover and care

    A final walkthrough, a snag list closed out, and support after move-in.
